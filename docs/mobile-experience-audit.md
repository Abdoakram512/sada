## Global Findings (`390×844`)
- **Total Page Height**: `28,509 px` (~`33.8×` mobile screens) due to unoptimized vertical card stacks in Scene 05 (`4,978 px`), Scene 10 (`3,831 px`), and Scene 09 (`3,700 px`).
- **Body Typography**: `pSize` renders at `15.2 px` (`0.95rem`), below the `16–17 px` comfortable Arabic mobile target.
- **Horizontal Overflow**: `0` (`scrollWidth === innerWidth` across `320px–1024px`).
- **Audio Dock & Subtitles**: Total height `167 px` (`asideTop: 666 px` on `844 px` height), occluding lower Hero characters & `يتحدث` badges; interactive buttons measure **`27×27 px`** to **`32×32 px`** (below `44×44 px` minimum touch target), and Volume slider button is `hidden sm:inline-flex` (`0×0 px` unreachable on mobile!).
- **Remaining Nested Phone Bezels**: `AppWorkflowSection.tsx` was already fixed, **but** `TrainingJourneySection.tsx` (`line 266`) and `ListeningNoiseSection.tsx` (`line 166`) still wrap pre-framed `screen_*.png` mockups inside dark outer CSS phone borders ("تليفونين في بعض").

## Transition Audit (10 Connections)
Currently [SceneTransition.tsx](file:///c:/Users/pc/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/sada-experience/src/components/SceneTransition.tsx) renders identical static SVG paths at fixed aspect ratios across mobile and desktop, appearing overly stretched horizontally on `390px` viewports with zero scroll-driven reveal. All 10 transitions will be upgraded with mobile-tuned geometry (`viewBox` proportion / crisp mobile stroke weights) and GPU-composited GSAP `matchMedia()` scroll reveals (`translate3d`, `scale`, `opacity`).
