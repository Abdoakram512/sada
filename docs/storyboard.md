# SADA Storyboard — «صدى — لكل صوت حكاية، ولكل طفل رحلة»

This storyboard defines the 11 continuous, interconnected storytelling scenes that transform SADA from a static graduation project page into an interactive animated educational film.

## Visual Motif: The Continuous Sound Ribbon
A single continuous animated harmonic wave flows behind and through every scene, morphing its role as the story progresses:
- **Scene 01 → 02**: Expands from behind the cast into everyday listening ripples.
- **Scene 02 → 03**: Coalesces into the SADA tablet/phone interface.
- **Scene 03 → 04**: Splits into six distinct frequency orbs (`/m/, /u/, /a/, /i/, /ʃ/, /s/`).
- **Scene 04 → 05**: Weaves into an ascending 5-stage adventure trail through the auditory skills.
- **Scene 05 → 06**: Layers into foreground target sound vs. gentle background environment ripples.
- **Scene 06 → 07**: Ignites into vibrant star particles entering the dark-indigo SADA Game World.
- **Scene 07 → 08**: Carries a golden progress star from the reward screen into the Family & Specialist hub.
- **Scene 08 → 09**: Straightens into a longitudinal assessment timeline (Pre → Follow-up → Post).
- **Scene 09 → 10**: Arcs around the 10 graduation project creators.
- **Scene 10 → 11**: Reunites all five characters for the final celebratory brand reveal.
